Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Vestrona handles information when you use the Vestrona iPhone application.

Effective date: October 28, 2026
The short version

Vestrona does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Your vesting schedules and related information remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Information We Collect

Vestrona does not collect personal information or usage data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking technologies, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter—such as token names, allocation amounts, vesting dates, cliff periods, unlock frequencies, notes, and reminder settings—is stored locally on your device. Vestrona does not upload this information to a developer-operated server.

You are responsible for maintaining access to your device and for retaining any exported copies you may need. Because we do not keep server copies, we cannot remotely access, restore, or delete your local information.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Vestrona uses Apple's local notification system to display unlock reminders on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

Exports and Sharing

When you choose to export or share information, the requested file is prepared on your device and passed to the destination you select through iOS. Vestrona does not receive a copy.

If you choose a third-party destination—such as an email provider, cloud storage service, or messaging app—its handling of the shared file is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

Wallets, Blockchains, and Financial Services

Vestrona does not connect to cryptocurrency wallets, access private keys or recovery phrases, read blockchain activity, execute transactions, or retrieve token prices. It is a planning and organization tool and does not provide financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.

Data Disclosure and Sale

Because Vestrona does not collect your data, we do not sell, rent, disclose, or use personal information for advertising or profiling.

Children's Privacy

Vestrona is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Security

Vestrona is designed to keep app information on your device. You should protect your device with an appropriate passcode and keep iOS up to date. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Vestrona's features or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If future app versions introduce data collection, the policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Vestrona's privacy practices, email vestrona.support@gmail.com.
