FAQ

Common questions.

If you still need help, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened when you contact us.

Does Vestrona connect to a wallet or blockchain?

No. Vestrona is a planning and organization tool. It does not connect to wallets, read on-chain activity, execute transactions, or provide token prices.

Where is my schedule information stored?

Your schedule information is stored locally on your device. Vestrona does not upload it to a developer-operated server.

Why did I not receive an unlock reminder?

Open iPhone Settings, find Vestrona, and confirm that notifications are allowed. Also verify the reminder date and time inside the schedule.

Can Vestrona recover deleted information?

No. Because Vestrona does not maintain user accounts or server copies, support cannot remotely recover information removed from your device.

Does Vestrona provide financial advice?

No. Information shown in the app is for personal planning and record-keeping only. It is not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.
